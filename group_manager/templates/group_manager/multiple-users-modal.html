Member list format

Each member needs to be defined by their email address. There should be one member per line.
A role can be included after a semicolon to be assigned automatically to the new member.
If no role is specified, the new member will be assigned to "member" role by default.

Example:
member1@yoda.uu.nl
member2@yoda.uu.nl;reader
member3@yoda.uu.nl
